02 / The mod

In development

JoJo
Heaven's Arrow.

A JoJo mod for Minecraft that I'm one of the animators on. It's not out yet, but when it drops I'll put the link right here.

These are the kinds of things I animate for it:

  1. 01

    Poses

    The iconic JoJo poses. Most of what you saw up there.

  2. 02

    Idles

    What a character does when they're just standing there, like the Approaching Me one.

  3. 03

    Stand attacks

    Barrages, abilities and all the flashy stuff.

  4. 04

    Player movement

    Walking, running, blocking. You see it all the time, so it has to feel right.
